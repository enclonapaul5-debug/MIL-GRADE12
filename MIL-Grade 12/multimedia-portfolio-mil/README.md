## 🎨 Interactive Exhibition Features Built-In

1. **Lobby Speech Synthesizer:** Click *"Listen to Statement"* to experience an audio-guided walkthrough of the curator's manifesto.
2. **Wing 1 4-Era Simulator:**
   - Play/pause 4-minute presentation with synchronized voiceover narration.
   - Click any era tab (Pre-Industrial, Industrial, Electronic, Information Age) to jump through media history.
   - Click *"Storyboard"* to inspect the detailed scene-by-scene script.
3. **Wing 2 Composition Analysis Overlays:**
   - Toggle **Rule of Thirds Grid** directly over photograph frames.
   - Toggle **Leading Lines Vectors** showing architectural ocular funnels.
   - Toggle **Focal Point Targets** highlighting the exact center of visual gravity.
4. **Wing 3 Typographic Specimen Studio:**
   - Switch between **Transitional Serif**, **Editorial Sans**, and **Academic Monospace**.
   - Adjust type scale between **A-**, **A**, and **A+**.
   - Toggle between **Dual-Column Newspaper Zine** and **Single-Column Reading Flow**.
5. **Wing 4 Studio Audio Deck:**
   - Real-time animated **oscilloscope waveform** generated via Canvas.
   - **SFX Soundboard:** Audition individual production cues (*Triple Notification Chime*, *Revenue Register*, *Analog Tape-Stop Dead Air*).
   - Auto-scrolling **studio teleprompter** synchronized with playback.
6. **Curatorial Placards:** Every wing features a dedicated brass-bordered museum plaque with interactive tabs comparing *Design Elements & Principles* against *Media Selection Criteria*.
7. **Exhibition Toolkit Drawer:**
   - One-click button to print/save the entire exhibition as an academic PDF.
   - Guidance for importing content into Google Sites, Canva, Notion, Wix, or WordPress.

## 🏫 Transferring to School Platforms

If your instructor or showcase requires submission on a specific website builder:

- **Google Sites:** Use the `PORTFOLIO_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` text directly across 5 pages (Lobby + 4 Wings), or use the "Embed" widget in Google Sites to embed your hosted `index.html`.
- **Canva Websites:** Use the verbatim text from `PORTFOLIO_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` and replicate the typography layout shown in Wing 3.
- **Notion:** Paste the markdown directly into a Notion page—it will automatically format all headings, blockquotes, callouts, and checklists!
- **Wix / WordPress:** Use the structured layout from `PORTFOLIO_MASTER_DOCUMENT.md` to populate your portfolio pages.
